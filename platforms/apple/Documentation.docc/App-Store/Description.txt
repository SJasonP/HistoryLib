A lightweight app for storing, browsing, and managing your browser history.

===== Why I Built This App =====

As a heavy Apple user, I've always used Safari as my one and only browser.
But after years of continuous use, junk and unnecessary data started piling up. Eventually, I had no choice but to back up my entire browsing history, give Safari a thorough cleanup, and then import everything back afterward.
Or so I thought...
When I reopened Safari and tried to restore my browsing history, I was completely caught off guard.
On macOS, Safari would randomly leave out bits and pieces of my history during import. And on iOS and iPadOS, things were even worse: no matter what I tried, only the most recent month's worth of history would get imported!
For someone who relies heavily on browser history, this was simply unacceptable. I'm not the kind of person who likes taking notes. Instead, whenever I need to find something again, I just dig through my old browsing history.
But it had become painfully clear that Safari—and the Apple ecosystem as a whole—simply wouldn't let me import my complete browsing history.
Obviously, I couldn't change Apple. So I decided to take matters into my own hands and build this app.

===== Features & Highlights =====

• Easy Imports: Effortlessly import browsing history from Safari, Google Chrome, and other browsers, no matter how far back it goes.
• Browse & Search: Freely explore your entire browsing history, with advanced search options whenever you need them.
• Flexible Exports: Export your data in multiple formats, making it easy to transfer to other devices or browsers.
• Automatic iCloud Sync: Keep your data in sync across all your Apple devices. Make a change once, and there's no need to manually import or export anything again.
• Offline & Open Source: Works completely offline, with all source code publicly available on GitHub. Your private data stays private.
• ...And More: There's plenty more waiting for you to discover!

===== When Might You Need This App? =====

• You want to browse, export, or manage your own browsing history.
• You're planning to clean up your browser but don't want to lose years of browsing history along the way.
• You already have exported browsing history stored locally, but can't find a good way to view it—and don't want to import it back into a browser.
• ...Or whatever other reason you might have for giving it a try!
